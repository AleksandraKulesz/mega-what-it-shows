#4471 merged every report about wrong dates into one ticket and marked it known: one bug, one fix. Read one by one, the reports describe more than one problem. Engineering closed #4471 on 5 Oct with a parser fix (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). That fix closes only the first row below.

#4471 is not one issue. Each problem below has its own ticket now.

  • hint: won’t fix. The booking-v3 form is retired.
  • one-day: no fix and no owner yet. The parser fix does not reach it. Its ticket stays open.

How each report was placed

  • parser: the tenant writes dates day first and the report did not come through the booking-v3 date form. Day and month swap: 7 April turns into 4 July.
  • hint: the report came through the booking-v3 form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type the month first, and the form reads it day first. The parser fix does not touch this form, and the form is retired.
  • one-day: the reports the desk already read as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The date moves by a day, or differs between Lisbon and Toronto. That is a time zone problem, not a swap. The desk files them under parser by tenant, so they are counted there as well and have their own row and ticket.

The tenant decides the cause, not the intake tag: intake tags hint from an en-US browser, which misses most hint reports and tags parser reports as hint. The export also repeats some reports across overlapping pages; every count here is one id, one report.

Lessons

  • A subject line says how a report feels, not what broke. Split by cause before you count.
  • A note the desk keeps on the side (oneDayMove) is a finding. Give it a row, not a footnote.
