How each report was placed

  • parser: the tenant writes dates day first and the report did not come through the booking-v3 date form. Day and month swap: 7 April turns into 4 July.
  • hint: the report came through the booking-v3 form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type the month first, and the form reads it day first. The parser fix does not touch this form.
  • one-day: the reports the desk already read as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The date moves by a day, or differs between Lisbon and Toronto. That is a time zone problem, not a swap, and CauseTable used to count these under parser.

The tenant decides the cause, not the intake tag: intake tags hint from an en-US browser, which misses most hint reports and tags parser reports as hint. The export also repeats some reports across overlapping pages; every count here is one id, one report.

Lessons

  • A subject line says how a report feels, not what broke. Split by cause before you count.
  • A note the desk keeps on the side (oneDayMove) is a finding. Give it a row, not a footnote.
